What to wear when you don't know what to wear

    First off, you don't need a good outfit to look cute or put together necessarily. There's a theory called the 2/3 method (clothes, Makeup, hair). As long as you're confident in 2 of the 3 appearances, you can look put together!! Now, clothes are a part of you that can always look good if you know how to style. It's probably the easiest of the three to get. So, when you have a bad hair day or you don't have time for makeup or something you can still look cute!!

    Clothing necessities

    -Jeans

    -Accessories (Jewelry, belt, sunglasses, scarf, bag, etc.)

    -comfortable top

    -sexy top

    -cute top

    -outerwear (Sweaters, jackets, flannel, button down)

    Jeans give a slightly more elevated look between comfy and casual, and it'll look much more "going out" look than wonderful but just comfy sweatpants. Plus baggy jeans could be just as comfortable as sweats if comfort is needed.

    Layers add complicity to your outfit, and looks like it was more effortful. That's why outerwear, depending on season, cannot be excluded from the fit because it's part of it. When you think "Oh it's cold out I need a coat." it shouldn't just be something to fix being cold but to be considered as you are making your fit.
